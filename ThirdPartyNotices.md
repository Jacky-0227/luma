## MobileVLCKit 3.7.3

- VLCKit source and license: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/VLCKit
- Official mirror: https://github.com/videolan/vlckit
- libVLC source: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/vlc
- Pinned podspec: https://github.com/CocoaPods/Specs/blob/master/Specs/b/f/7/MobileVLCKit/3.7.3/MobileVLCKit.podspec.json

The repository publishes Luma source and build instructions. The Podfile can be changed to rebuild/relink with a modified compatible VLCKit.
